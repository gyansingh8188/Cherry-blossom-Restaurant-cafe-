Our Culinary Heritage

A Place Where Flavours Meet Beautiful Moments.

Nestled on the 1st Floor of Jasraj Kripa Building in Robertsganj, Cherry Blossom Restaurant & Cafe was founded with a singular devotion: creating a refined space where families, friends, and travellers can slow down and celebrate authentic food.

From sizzling Indian tandoor delicacies and zesty Asian wok favorites to artisanal shakes and comforting cafe fare, every dish is prepared with fresh produce, authentic spices, and genuine care.
